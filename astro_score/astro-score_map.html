AstroScore map

One day, every saved spot, on a map. Each place carries a cut-down copy of the cell astro-score_daily.html draws for it — the two night blocks (00–05 · 19–23), each just its AstroScore bar and blue ClearScore strip (the rest of the daily cell is in the hover text) — but for the one date in the time box rather than a fortnight of columns, laid out where the place actually is. The daily grid answers which place, which night; this answers where, tonight. The blue-ringed card is 輸入, the one that follows the location box. Four forecast models come back in every request; the tabs pick which one the bars are scored from. No API key needed.

how to read a card (說明)
  • Each card is one date at one place: left stack = the small hours (00–05) after midnight starting that date, right stack = that evening. A night therefore spans two dates — tonight is the right stack today and the left stack tomorrow.
  • Green bar = the block's best AstroScore hour (full > 90, ¾ > 70, ½ > 50, ¼ > 30, blank at or below); a faint · means no forecast for that block.
  • Strip under it: ClearScore (best dark-hour 100 − Cloud, the moon left out). Deeper is better; deep blue under a short bar is a clear night lost to moonlight.
  • Hover a cell for the exact numbers — MilkyScore, OrionScore, A*/M42/moon altitudes and the moon phase are there; the full cell is on astro-score_daily.html. Click a card to bring it to the front; click its name to open the hour-by-hour page for it.
  • bortle: a saved spot's is the figure kept in places.js; the 輸入 card's is computed — the decimal “app Bortle” of David Lorenz's World Atlas of Artificial Night Sky Brightness (from NOAA/VIIRS) at that point, as on astro-score_readable.html.
  • Cards are nudged off their pin (with a line back to it) when they would cover another; zoom in to untangle a cluster.
