AstroScore daily

The whole week for every saved spot at once: a row per place, a column per day, and each cell a four-block sparkline of the night. Same 觀星 score as astro-score_readable.html — this page just asks it of all nine saved places and folds each day's 24 hours into two half-day blocks. Read it to pick where and when, then open the readable grid for the hour-by-hour detail. No API key needed.

  • Each block is half a day, so each holds exactly one dark stretch: the small hours fall in 00–11 and the evening in 12–23, while their daylight halves score 0 and cannot touch a maximum.
  • A night straddles midnight, so it reads across two cells — the right glyph of one day (that evening) and the left glyph of the next (its small hours). █ on the right of 08/18 next to █ on the left of 08/19 is one clear night. A single cell showing ██ is instead the tail of the night just gone beside the head of the one coming.
  • The best hour, not a total or an average — one clear hour is worth the drive even when the rest of the night clouds over, and a sum would average it away. It also means a bar says nothing about how long the window lasts: open the readable grid for that.
  • 從今天開始 hides whole days before today; it never shortens a day it shows. Today's column always covers the full 24 hours, hours already gone included, so a block does not shrink as the afternoon wears on. Untick it to bring back whatever past_days asked for. Hover any cell for the exact sums.
  • The score itself is (100 − 雲量) × (100 − 月亮扣分) / 100, zeroed while the sun is above −10°, 月亮扣分 ramping 0 → 100 as the moon climbs 0° → 10°. Cloud is icon_global; sun and moon are computed in the page. See astro-score_readable.html for the worked examples.
