moon phase & altitude

Is the moon up, and how bright? A time and a lat,lon in, the 月亮 and 月相 rows of astro-score_readable out, plus the 月亮扣分 they feed into — with every intermediate shown. No API call: Open-Meteo offers daily=moon_phase and nothing hourly, so this is Meeus' abridged lunar series — 14 longitude terms, 8 latitude, 4 distance, good to roughly 0.3°. The phase needs the sun too, so that page's series is here as well.
