sun altitude

Is it dark yet? A time and a lat,lon in, the sun's altitude out — the 太陽 row of astro-score_readable and the gate that zeroes its 觀星 score, with every intermediate shown. No API call: Open-Meteo returns only sunrise and sunset, so the angle in between is computed locally from Meeus' low-precision solar series — two sine terms, good to about 0.01°.
