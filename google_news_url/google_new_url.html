google_new_url

Browser port of google_new_url.py / google_new_url.mjs, wrapped around the search feed the README describes: fetch news.google.com/rss/search for a keyword, sort the items by <pubDate> (newest first), take the first N, and resolve every <link> redirect to the real article URL. Only the URL is resolved — no article content is read.

Needs your own CORS proxy. news.google.com sends no Access-Control-Allow-Origin, so the browser cannot call it directly — every request below goes through the proxy you fill in. It must forward POST as well as GET: the feed and the article pages are GETs, but the batchexecute resolve is a POST, and Google answers 405 to a GET there. Resolving one link means downloading its Google News article page (~570 KB) to scrape the signature, so 20 links pull ~12 MB through that proxy. The three proxy fields are held for the request and nothing else, unless you tick 記住 proxy 設定 — then all three, the password included, are kept in this browser's localStorage until you untick it. Nothing is ever sent anywhere but the proxy itself.
